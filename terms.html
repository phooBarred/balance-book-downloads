Terms of use

Updated November 1, 2026

Balance Book is a personal recordkeeping tool. It records the amounts and agreements you enter and helps prepare messages. It does not transfer money, send messages automatically, or determine your legal or financial obligations.

Review your records

Check entered amounts, portions, dates, and message drafts before relying on or sharing them. Marking an update as sent is your own record; it is not confirmation of delivery from a messaging service.

Backups and updates

Google backup is optional and depends on permission, available storage, internet access, and Android scheduling. Check backup status before relying on recovery. Install updates over the existing app to preserve its local records.

External services

Google, GitHub, Android, and the messaging app you select operate their respective services under their own terms. Balance Book's data handling is explained in its privacy policy.
