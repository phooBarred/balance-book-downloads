BALANCE BOOK · ANDROID

Shared expenses.
A clear balance.

A personal ledger for shared expenses, payments, and child support deductions.

Record who paid, choose each person's portion, and prepare a balance update to share in your preferred messaging app. Your records work offline on your phone.

Optional Google backup

Choose your Google account to back up and restore your ledger. Google Drive backup stores an app-specific copy in your account without a folder picker. Google asks for permission, and you choose when to start backing up.

Android downloads ↗

Install updates over your existing app to keep your records. Do not uninstall or clear app data to update.
