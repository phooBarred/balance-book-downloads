Privacy policy

Updated November 1, 2026 · Android app

Your phone's records

Balance Book stores the ledger you enter on your phone, including names, amounts, descriptions, dates, saved messages, and ledger settings such as a contact's phone number. The Android app works offline and does not automatically send messages or transfer money.

Google account and backup

Google backup is optional. Version 0.8.11 and later request access to an app-specific hidden Google Drive storage area and your Google account email and identifier. The app uses that identity to show the selected account and keep each account's backup separate. It does not request access to your other Drive files.

When you choose to back up, the app uploads a copy of your ledger to that storage area. If you enable a schedule, it attempts backups at or after your selected time when Android and your connection permit. Restore reads the saved copy and asks before replacing this phone's records. Account selection alone does not upload or restore your ledger.

Google Play services handles authorization. Access tokens are used in native memory for Google requests; the app does not store your Google password or a server refresh token. Data travels over HTTPS. Google provides the account and storage services under its own policies.

Earlier backups

Android releases before 0.8.11 used Google sign-in with a Firebase backup service. Historical copies may remain there for recovery. They are not automatically moved to Drive or deleted by an app update. The app maintainer can assist with recovery or deletion of these older copies using the support contact shown on the Google authorization screen.

Sharing and updates

When you choose Copy + open, your draft is copied to Android's clipboard and the selected messaging app is opened. You control sending; that messaging app's privacy practices apply. App updates are checked and downloaded from GitHub when you request them, and Android handles installation approval. These services receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address.

Your choices

Turn off scheduled backups or sign out in Settings → Google backup. Signing out leaves the local ledger and saved Google backup intact. You can revoke the app's Google access in your Google account and manage its hidden app data through Google Drive's app settings. Removing the app or its local data can remove the phone's records; confirm that a usable backup exists first.

Other uses and this website

The Android app contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not sell ledger data or use it to train AI models. The public pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, use no analytics scripts or forms, and do not receive your ledger. GitHub may process technical request information to operate its hosting service. This policy describes the Android app; any separate private web installation has its own storage.

Contact

Use the developer support email shown on Balance Book's Google authorization screen for privacy, backup recovery, or deletion requests. Do not post financial records or account credentials in public GitHub discussions.
